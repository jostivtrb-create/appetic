# Logo de Leñita Casera

Tipo **A + C** (con texto, sobre croma verde para recortar). Una sola pieza: luego se despieza en local
(regla aprendida): el **logo completo** va a `public/locales/lenita-casera/logo.webp` (cabecera
sobre madera oscura, skin `lena`) y **solo el símbolo** (troncos + llama) a `icono.webp` cuadrado.

- Fondo croma: **verde `#00FF00`** (el logo no lleva verdes).
- Letras crema con contorno café grueso: se leen sobre la madera oscura de la cabecera Y sobre fondos claros (lista del inicio).
- Paleta del local: llama `#E2621B`, brasa `#F4A62A`, madera `#5A2A12`, madera honda `#3B1B0B`, crema `#FFF1DC`.

## Prompt

```
A professional restaurant logo for a Colombian home-style wood-fired grill called "LEÑITA CASERA". At the top, the emblem: three short split firewood logs stacked in a small crossed pile, warm wood brown (#5A2A12) with lighter end rings showing the wood grain, and a lively, generous flame rising from them in layered tongues of fire orange (#E2621B), ember yellow (#F4A62A) and a deep red core, with two or three tiny sparks. Directly below the emblem, the name in two stacked lines, perfectly centered: the word "LEÑITA" large, and the word "CASERA" slightly smaller underneath. LEÑITA is spelled L-E-Ñ-I-T-A, with a clear tilde (~) on top of the N; do not write "LENITA". The lettering is a chunky, friendly, rounded slab-serif with a hand-crafted rustic feel, filled with warm cream (#FFF1DC), with a thick dark wood-brown outline (#3B1B0B) and a short solid fire-orange (#E2621B) 3D extrusion offset to the lower right, so it reads well on both dark and light backgrounds. Under the name, a small dark wood-brown ribbon banner with the text "RESTAURANTE · PARRILLA" in small cream capital letters. Style: polished flat vector illustration with subtle shading, bold clean shapes, like a premium hand-painted wooden sign, warm and homemade, not childish. Vertical stacked composition, fully visible and centered, with about 12% empty margin on every side, nothing cropped; keep the bottom-right corner empty. Crisp clean edges, no motion blur, no depth of field. On a perfectly flat, solid pure green (#00FF00) background, uniform edge to edge, with no gradient, no texture, no vignette, no floor. No drop shadow, no cast shadow, no glow, no reflection, no white outline or sticker border around the logo. Square 1:1. Render only the quoted text, exactly as written, with correct Spanish spelling; do not draw any of these instructions as text. No other text, no watermark.
```
